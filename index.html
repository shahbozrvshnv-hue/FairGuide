THE PROBLEM WE SOLVE

Traveling somewhere new should feel exciting — not uncertain.

Uzbekistan is one of the world’s most welcoming countries. But as an international visitor, you may not speak Uzbek or Russian — and you may have no way of knowing whether a price you’re quoted is a reasonable one.

So you depend on a guide or translator to communicate with local businesses. That dependency creates an information imbalance — a gap between what locals know and what you know.

The transparency gap Simplified example
Actual / reference price 10,000 UZS
Price presented to tourist 20,000 UZS
Difference +10,000 UZS

FairGuide is built to give you greater transparency, so you can make better-informed decisions. Prices can legitimately vary by place and season — a difference alone never means fraud. Where reliable data exists, FairGuide simply gives you a fair reference to compare against.

FAIRGUIDE TECHNOLOGY

Know the price before you pay.

FairGuide helps tourists compare available product information and reference prices, giving them more confidence when shopping in Uzbekistan.

  1. 1
    Point your camera

    Scan a barcode, a product, a price tag — or simply take a photo.

  2. 2
    See available reference information

    FairGuide compares what it finds with reference price data collected for that product and region.

  3. 3
    Decide with confidence

    You see the difference clearly — and decide what feels fair to you.

Reference prices may vary by store, location, and time. FairGuide does not determine whether a seller is fraudulent solely from a price difference.

TRUST & SAFETY

Built around trust.

FairGuide is designed to build a trusted environment for both tourists and local users — with safeguards that grow alongside the community.

Verified profiles

Guides and translators go through identity and credential checks before appearing on the platform.

Transparent reviews

Honest, experience-based reviews help great locals shine — and keep everyone accountable.

Secure communication

Chat and booking stay inside FairGuide, with your information protected.

Reporting system

See something wrong? Report it in one tap — our team reviews every case.

Community standards

Clear rules of fairness and respect that every member of the community agrees to.

Future identity verification

Stronger identity tools are planned as the platform and its community grow.

No platform can promise that nothing bad will ever happen. We can promise constant work: better verification, faster response, and a community that looks out for each other.
